Quick start guide
Part 1 Install it once
You were sent a single file named Volksmond.zip. There is nothing to install in the usual sense. You unzip it, then run the app straight from the folder.
1
Unzip the folder
Right-click Volksmond.zip and choose Extract All. Pick a normal place to keep it, like your Documents folder. Open the folder it creates. Run the app from there, not from inside the zip preview.
2
Open the app
In the unzipped folder, double-click Volksmond.exe. The Volksmond window opens on its own. The other files next to it are part of the app, you can leave them alone.
3
If Windows shows a blue warning
You may see Windows protected your PC. Click More info, then Run anyway. This appears only because the app is new and not yet signed by a certificate. It is the same file you were sent, and it is safe. You will not see this again on this computer.
4
Answer the short setup
A welcome screen greets you, then asks one question: do you want to just transcribe, or also summarise on your machine. For summaries, pick a model size and Volksmond downloads it for you in one click. You can skip this and turn summaries on later in Settings.
One thing needs the internet, once.
The first time you transcribe, Volksmond downloads the speech model it runs on (this is a one-time download and it needs an internet connection that one time). After that, transcription and summaries run entirely on your computer, offline.
Choosing a summary model
Two sizes, both free and both run on your machine. Gemma 4 (2 billion) is lighter and faster, and works well on most computers. Gemma 4 (4 billion) is a little more polished and needs more memory. If unsure, start with the 2 billion.

Upload a recording
Pick an audio or video file you already have. Volksmond transcribes it locally, just like a live meeting.
Record only, later
For machines that cannot keep up live. Record the audio cleanly now, then transcribe it when you are back at a desk.
Running a live meeting
Set it up, then press Begin
Give the meeting a title, choose the language (Afrikaans, English, or Auto-detect) and a quality level. Adding the names and jargon you expect to hear genuinely improves accuracy, especially for Afrikaans and the mix. All of this is optional.
Two audio sources
Your microphone picks up your voice. System audio picks up everyone else, the sound coming out of your computer. So Volksmond captures both sides of a Teams, Zoom, or Meet call you have joined as a guest. Tip: wear headphones, so the microphone does not re-hear the other people and transcribe them twice.
Watch it, then stop and save
The transcript appears live as people talk. When you are done, press Stop and save. If you were also recording the audio, a small menu lets you stop the recording, the transcript, or both.

Part 2 of 2 · Use
After a meeting
Open, read, or copy it
The transcript is saved as a plain text file. From the finished screen you can open the folder, open the transcript to read it, or copy it all to paste elsewhere.
Summarise it, on your machine
If you turned summaries on, press Summarise for the decisions, the to-dos, and what stayed open. Choose the summary in English or Afrikaans. It runs locally and saves next to the transcript.
Find it again in History
The History tab lists every past meeting. Search it, open any transcript to re-read or summarise, and re-transcribe any recording you kept.

Settings worth knowing
Make it yours
Appearance: light, dark, or follow Windows. Interface language: switch the whole app between English and Afrikaans. Default context: save the names and jargon you use often, applied to every meeting.
Where your files live
Under Data and privacy you can change the folder transcripts save to. For maximum privacy, pick a folder that a cloud service (OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox) does not sync. Audio recording stays off unless you switch it on for a meeting.
Your privacy, in plain terms
Nothing you say or transcribe is ever sent off your computer. Volksmond has no accounts and no telemetry, and the offline edition has no way to reach the internet once the models are in place. The source code is available so anyone can confirm it never phones home.
Need a hand
Report a bug or an idea
Use Report a bug or idea at the bottom of the side menu. It opens a pre-filled email, nothing is sent automatically. Write to volksmond@digiphyte.com.
